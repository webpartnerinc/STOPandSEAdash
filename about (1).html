Our story

Seafood, the friendly way

Stop and Sea is a family-friendly seafood restaurant at 909 S Main St in Bellefontaine, Ohio. The idea is simple: honest, hand-breaded fish and shrimp, burgers and chicken, with hand-cut fries and homemade coleslaw and potato salad on the side.

Inside, it is a cozy, seated diner where the food is made fresh when you order and arrives hot. Guests often call it a hidden gem, and the owner and staff are known for stopping to chat and for taking dietary needs seriously.

Our logo says it all: a fish skeleton set with a fork, knife and spoon. Clean plate, happy guests.

Quick answers

Do you offer carryout?

Yes. Call (937) 599-3000 to place a carryout order.

Are there gluten-free options?

Yes, gluten-free fish, shrimp and fries are available. Please tell your server about any allergies or dietary needs.

Is there more than seafood?

Absolutely. Burgers, chicken, salads and hand-cut fries with homemade slaw and potato salad are all part of the lineup.

Specials are posted on our Facebook page, so check there before you head over.
